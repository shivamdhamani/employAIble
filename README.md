## What is EmployAIable?

EmployAIable is **not** another job portal for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs).

It is the **missing intelligence layer** that computes:

> **Candidate Capability × Job Requirement × Workplace Accessibility × Local Infrastructure × Accommodation × Cost × Retention Probability**

Built specifically for **Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities in India** — where 69% of India's 2.68 crore PwDs actually live — and the informal/MSME economy that existing platforms (SwarajAbility, Atypical Advantage) structurally cannot reach.

---

## The Core Problem

Every existing platform — government or private — is optimized for a candidate who is:
- Urban & well-connected
- Already certified / skilled
- Being placed into a large formal employer

**None of them solve for a candidate who is:**
- Rural / Tier 2-3
- Intermittently connected
- Being placed into an informal/MSME employer
- In a town with no accessibility data and no nearby vocational-rehab touchpoint

---

## Key Features

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Sustainable Placement Score (SPP)** | 7-dimension match: skill × travel × accessibility × AT-compat × accommodation × cost × retention |
| **Functional Capability Profiling** | Not "I know Excel" — but "I can use Excel via screen reader IF the CRM supports keyboard navigation" |
| **Local Employment Opportunity Map** | AI tells district admin: "31 candidates can do bookkeeping, 18 employer slots available in Ajmer" |
| **"Convert This Job" Wizard** | Turns any existing job role into a disability-inclusive role for MSMEs |
| **Accommodation Cost Estimator** | Estimates cost per accommodation + surfaces RPWD Act subsidies |
| **CSC / Panchayat Onboarding** | Hindi-first, voice-guided, offline-capable flow for rural PwDs |

---

## 5 Portals

1. **Candidate Portal** — Functional capability profiling + SPP-scored job matches
2. **Employer Portal** — Convert-This-Job wizard + incentive discovery
3. **Government Dashboard** — District-level demand map + quota compliance tracker
4. **Match Scoring Engine** — Interactive 7-dimension feasibility scorer
5. **CSC / Panchayat Node** — Offline-first, Hindi-first rural onboarding
